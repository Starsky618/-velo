What VELO does

Road cycling, without becoming a general fitness platform.

VELO stays focused on real outdoor road cycling. We do not build hardware, sell equipment, or provide indoor virtual riding.

01

Ride history

Import FIT and GPX files or connect Strava. VELO shows only the performance fields present in the source data.

02

Segment results

Review results and personal changes on defined road-cycling segments without filling gaps with invented values.

03

Routes and route books

Browse, draw, save, and export GPX or TCX route books for use on a bike computer or companion app.

Garmin data sync

Authorize once. Bring each ride back.

Garmin Connect sync is in the application and development stage. It is not yet available to VELO users.

1

2
